Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Rise Remotely ("we," "us," or "our") is operated by Christina Voll, Unipessoal LDA, a company registered in Portugal. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, quiz, and related services, and how we use it.

What we collect

When you take the Rise Remotely archetype quiz, join our waitlist, or sign up for our mailing list, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your quiz responses and resulting archetype, scores, and related results
  • Basic technical information such as browser type and general location, collected automatically for security and analytics purposes

How we use your information

  • To send you your quiz results and related content
  • To send emails about Rise Remotely products, including the Business Blueprint, courses, and the coliving bootcamp
  • To improve our quiz, website, and products
  • To respond to you if you contact us directly

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Email marketing

We use Kit (formerly ConvertKit) to manage our mailing list and send emails. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time. When you unsubscribe, your information is removed from future mailings.

Third-party services

We use the following third-party services to operate Rise Remotely, each of which has its own privacy practices:

  • Kit — email delivery and subscriber management
  • Railway — website and application hosting
  • Stripe — payment processing, where applicable

Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at info@riseremotely.com. If you are located in the EU, UK, or Switzerland, you have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as you remain subscribed to our mailing list or have an active account with us, and for a reasonable period afterward as required for legal, accounting, or business purposes.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your information, contact us at info@riseremotely.com.
